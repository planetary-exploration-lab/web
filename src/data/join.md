We're currently taking undergraduate researchers. For graduate students, we're open to considering an exceptional candidate for fall 2027 but aren't actively recruiting. We aren't actively recruiting postdocs. Fellowships are welcome but not necessary.

If your situation doesn't match any of these categories, or you're not sure you meet every expectation, reach out anyway. Unusual paths are welcome here.

## How to reach out

For undergraduates, graduate students, and postdocs: email Prof. Carr (the address is in the [Georgia Tech directory](https://ae.gatech.edu/directory/person/christopher-carr)) with a subject line that starts "PXL undergraduate", "PXL graduate", or "PXL postdoc", and include:

- what interests you about our work, and which projects fit;
- the type of position you're looking for;
- a CV or résumé;
- a writing sample (a paper or lab report) and, if relevant, a link to a portfolio;
- short answers to two questions: *What's the most creative thing you've done with AI?* and *What's one thing you don't use AI for, on purpose?*

## High school students

We don't host high school students in the lab, but there's a lot you can do now.

- **Build skills.** Learn to code, design and build something, read about space science, and write up what you did.
- **Take on a challenge.**
  - [Genes in Space](https://www.genesinspace.org/) (grades 7–12; the winning experiment flies on the International Space Station)
  - [NASA Space Apps Challenge](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/) (under 18 with a parent or guardian)
  - [NASA TechRise Student Challenge](https://www.nasa.gov/stmd-flight-opportunities/access-flight-tests/techrise/) (teams in grades 6–12)
  - [American Rocketry Challenge](https://rocketrychallenge.org/)
- **Join an established program.**
  - [NASA internships](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/internship-programs/) (16 and older)
  - NASA's [STEM Enhancement in Earth Science (SEES)](https://www.csr.utexas.edu/sees-internship/) summer internship
  - Georgia Tech's [K–12 programs at the Center for Education Integrating Science, Mathematics, and Computing (CEISMC)](https://ceismc.gatech.edu/)

## Undergraduates

- **Georgia Tech students:** contact Prof. Carr first, and we'll work out the details. For aerospace engineering (AE) students, this comes before an [AE research permit](https://ae.gatech.edu/research-permits). The [Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP)](https://urop.gatech.edu/) has more about undergraduate research at Georgia Tech.
- **Students at other universities:** Georgia Tech's [Summer Undergraduate Research in Engineering (SURE)](https://sure.gatech.edu/) program, National Science Foundation (NSF) Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) sites, and NASA internships.

## Graduate students

Admission is through Georgia Tech [graduate admissions](https://grad.gatech.edu/degree-programs/aerospace-engineering). If you'd like to develop a fellowship application together, such as the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP) or NASA Future Investigators in NASA Earth and Space Science and Technology (FINESST), reach out 3–6 months before the deadline.

## Postdocs

If you're interested in working with us, consider applying to the [AE Postdoctoral Fellows Program](https://ae.gatech.edu/post-doc-fellows) or another fellowship, such as the [NASA Postdoctoral Program](https://science.nasa.gov/nasa-postdoc-program) (hosting rules vary; we can check eligibility together), and talk with Prof. Carr about it first. Or reach out to discuss other options.
