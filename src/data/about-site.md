## Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What we do:

- Every page is checked automatically on every change, in light and dark color schemes, for WCAG 2.0, 2.1, and 2.2 Level A and AA rules, and on a screen 320 pixels wide.
- We also review each type of page for keyboard use, 200% zoom, and increased text spacing.
- Images have text descriptions, and link text says where each link goes. Any video we host must have captions; the site does not build without them.
- The site respects your system settings for dark mode and reduced motion.

Known limitations:

- Links to other websites, such as news coverage and publishers' pages, lead to content we do not control. Some publishers' PDFs may not be fully accessible.
- Translations into other languages are not yet available; those pages show English text.

### Report a barrier

If something on this site does not work for you, or you need information in another format, use Georgia Tech's [Report a Digital Accessibility Issue](https://digitalaccess.gatech.edu/report-digital-accessibility-issue) process. Please include the page address and what went wrong. Georgia Tech's [Digital Access](https://digitalaccess.gatech.edu/) site has more about the Institute's accessibility commitment.

## Privacy

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other websites when you open a page. Links to maps, publishers, and news outlets take you to those sites, which have their own policies.
