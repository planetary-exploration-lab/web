We are working to develop the Electronic Life-detection Instrument for Enceladus/Europa (ELIE). The instrument is capable of detecting prebiotic, ancient, or extant life, and of distinguishing forward contamination, through detection of two universal biomarkers: the amino acid abundance distribution and informational polymers. We will validate single-molecule nanogap detection and develop a breadboard system to advance ELIE from technology readiness level (TRL) 2 to TRL 4.

For more information, see the [Media Lab project overview](https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/ELI/overview/) and the [Media Lab project updates](https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/ELI/updates/) about parabolic flights conducted as part of a collaboration with the [MIT Media Lab Space Exploration Initiative](https://www.media.mit.edu/groups/space-exploration/overview/).
