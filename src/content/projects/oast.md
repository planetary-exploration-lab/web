The [Oceans Across Space and Time (OAST) project](https://schmidt.astro.cornell.edu/current-project-oast/) is part of the [Network for Life Detection (NfoLD)](https://www.nfold.org/), funded by [NASA’s Astrobiology Program](https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/). Our goal is to understand how ocean worlds and their biospheres co-evolve to produce detectable signals of a past or present living world, by combining the expertise of a multidisciplinary team. This work has involved studies of hypersaline lakes in interior British Columbia, a commercial salt works (South Bay Salt Works, San Diego), a set of Western Australia transient lakes, and a robotic mission to a Deep Hypersaline Anoxic Basin (DHAB). Our lab’s part of the work involves microbial adaptation and evolution to hypersaline systems, focusing on life detection, for example with biological and solid-state nanopores.
