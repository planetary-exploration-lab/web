This research focused on developing a comprehensive understanding of a hypersaline, Mars analog environment, addressing the central question: “What types of biosignatures form and are preserved over time?” We addressed this question specifically in a system dominated by magnesium sulfate, because sulfate salts have been widely documented on Mars but are not the dominant type here on Earth. The field site is located in and around Clinton, British Columbia, Canada. Our team worked at the Basque Lakes, Last Chance Lake, and Salt Lake.

For more information, see [Astrobioalex.com’s site](https://www.astrobioalex.com/copy-of-impact-cratering).
